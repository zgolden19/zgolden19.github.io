Privacy policy

Bowl Splitters Uploader · effective November 25, 2026

Bowl Splitters Uploader ("the app") is a Mac app used by the Bowl Splitters bowling team to publish the team's own league-night videos to the team's YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to your channel, set their title, description and privacy, and read back their status. It requests the youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and youtube scopes (the last only to change a video's privacy setting).
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): with your permission, the app publishes posts, Reels and photos to the Facebook Page and Instagram account you choose.
  • Local files: the videos and graphics on your Mac that you choose to publish.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. The app has no server. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and does not use it for advertising or analytics. Data goes only to the platform you are publishing to (Google/YouTube or Meta).

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Press Disconnect on the app's Accounts screen to delete the stored tokens from your Mac. You can also revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings or in Facebook's Business Integrations settings.
