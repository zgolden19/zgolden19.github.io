Bowl Splitters

Three bowlers. Three games. Every week.

Bowl Splitters is a league bowling team. We film our league nights and share the best shots, spares and finishes on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

Bowl Splitters Uploader

Bowl Splitters Uploader is our own Mac app. It cuts each league night into short videos and publishes them to the team's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is used only by the team to post the team's own videos.
